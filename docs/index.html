ACT 02 / ARCHITECTURAL RIGOR

The Ecological Fallacy & Context Isolation

The four datasets share zero primary keys. Forcing a SQL join across unlinked tables creates fabricated synthetic humans and catastrophic leakage. We enforce absolute isolation.

✕ The Amateur Fallacy: Naive Row Joins

Merging 15,841 market postings with 139 internal profiles based on text job titles produces hundreds of thousands of pseudo-records. It assumes every external posting reflects internal promotion criteria, inducing massive leakage.

RESULT: Severe Ecological Fallacy • Untenable Statistical Leakage
✓ Our Architecture: Context-Isolated Evidence Lanes

Each dataset runs inside an isolated computation lane. We extract statistical parameter vectors (log-odds and demand shares) independently, merging findings solely at the abstract construct level into an audited Evidence Registry.

RESULT: Zero Row-Level Leakage • Mathematically Sound Synthesis
ACT 03 / PRIMARY FINDING

The Demand-Reward Inversion

Comparing open-market keyword demand against internal promotion rewards revealed an inverse relationship: the skills most heavily filtered by job portals deliver the lowest return for internal career advancement.

Market Demand vs. Internal Reward
Core Algorithmic Coding (Python/SQL) Demand: 95.8% | Reward: +0.61
Mathematics & Statistics Rigor Demand: ~0.1% | Reward: +1.82
Executive Storytelling & Visualization Demand: ~0.1% | Reward: +1.35
The Strategic Insight

Coding is a Prerequisite, Not a Multiplier: Python and SQL act as table stakes to enter the applicant pool. However, once inside the enterprise, promotion decisions are driven by mathematical modeling (+1.82 Log-Odds) and storytelling capability (+1.35 Log-Odds).

Interaction Test: Technical × Storytelling interaction coefficient = +0.087 ($p=0.148$).
